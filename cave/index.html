A story, not a lesson. for those with a glass, and for those without

Each Sunday one wine comes onto the slate at the zinc, and one cheese with it. Each new wine has a card here: what it is, who made it and why that matters, what is in the glass, and what to eat beside it.

The newest is at the top. The earlier ones stay, so the cards add up to a book of the wines we chose.
