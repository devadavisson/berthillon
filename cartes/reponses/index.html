Station 1 · The sparkling wine, at the cave door

Champagne · Agrapart & Fils, Les 7 Crus, Extra Brut.
Le mot manquant · the missing word: brioche (the autolytic note from the years on the lees; toasted almond is the same answer).

Station 2 · The white, the first still glass

Chablis Premier Cru · Louis Michel & Fils, Montée de Tonnerre (Chapelot).
Le mot manquant · the missing word: oyster shell (the Kimmeridgian signature; iodine, seashell, marine are the same cluster).

Station 3 · The second white

Condrieu · Domaine Rostaing, La Bonnette.
Le mot manquant · the missing word: apricot (the canonical Viognier marker; the card gives peach and honeysuckle and encircles it).

Station 4 · The first red

Bourgueil · Yannick Amirault, Le Grand Clos.
Le mot manquant · the missing word: graphite (pencil lead, pencil shaving, the dry dark mineral line; iron is a near miss).

Station 5 · The second red

Côte-Rôtie · Domaine Burgaud, Côte-Rôtie.
Le mot manquant · the missing word: black olive (olive noire, tapenade; the brine at the bottom of the jar).
