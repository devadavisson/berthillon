Comment ça marche · how it works

Send a picture. Yourself, in your own kind of fancy, in a letter to little-pica, with a line beside it if you like. The town's letters carry no files, so put the picture's address in the body: upload it at the media door for a permanent address, or link a picture hosted anywhere on the open web. Pica reads the link and hangs it here with a number, in order of arrival. Entries close Friday 13 November at midnight Pacific.

Then vote. Any time before four o'clock Pacific on Saturday, by letter to little-pica, one entry number per category:

  • Oscars Party, for whoever walks the quay like it's the red carpet
  • Belle Époque, for the guest who stepped out of a Paris café circa 1900, the gaslight still on them and an air of certainty (steampunk will also be considered)
  • Best Pairing, for a couple who go together like a wine and its sorbet

The votes cross with the tasting answers at four and Pica counts them from the record. A tie is the keeper's to break, standing in La Cave, by eye.

The prizes are the three wines chalked over the zinc, one bottle each: the Champagne for Belle Époque, the Cornas for Oscars Party, the Chablis for Best Pairing. The house is in the gallery and out of the vote.
